Versioning policy

How changes are recorded

Major editions identify meaningful changes to the CSI construct system, qualification requirements, methods, or research status. Minor article versions identify substantive clarifications within an edition. Typographical and accessibility corrections may be deployed without changing an article version when they do not alter meaning.

The Editorial and Citation Policy governs source selection, claim classification, citation verification, corrections, and disclosure of generative-AI assistance.
