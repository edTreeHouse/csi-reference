How to use the Reference

Learn CSI through its foundational concepts.

Scholarly papers establish and test the research. The CSI Reference organizes that knowledge into permanent, accessible articles that can be refined as definitions, evidence, and methods mature.

Edition 0.1 remains the fixed ten-article archival release. New methodological articles are clearly identified as living extensions and do not alter the archived edition.

The About the CSI Research Program page documents authorship and status. The Editorial and Citation Policy governs claims and corrections. The Scholarly Sources registry and Citation Verification Audit provide verifiable support.
