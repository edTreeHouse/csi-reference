# TDR-000 — Establish a Canonical CSI Theory Handbook

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

The CSI research program shall maintain a canonical Theory Handbook that defines foundational concepts independently of any individual publication.

## Scientific Rationale

Research papers are archival snapshots of theory at a particular stage of development. A cumulative research framework requires a stable conceptual source that can evolve transparently while preserving consistent terminology and relationships across papers.

## Alternatives Considered

### Define CSI independently in each paper

Rejected because repeated redefinition increases the risk of conceptual drift, inconsistent terminology, and ambiguity about which formulation is canonical.

## Consequences

Future papers should derive foundational concepts from the Theory Handbook and identify deliberate deviations or extensions explicitly.

The handbook itself should maintain version history and links to relevant Theory Decision Records.

## Backward Compatibility

Backward compatible. Existing papers remain archival statements of the framework at their respective publication stages.
