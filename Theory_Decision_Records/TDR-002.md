# TDR-002 — Adopt Representation Neutrality

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

CSI shall not presume any evidence representation—including aggregation, relational modeling, temporal modeling, embeddings, or multimodal fusion—to be either sufficient or insufficient a priori.

## Scientific Rationale

Earlier CSI wording risked implying that aggregation is inherently incapable of representing collective states. The more defensible position is that representation adequacy depends on the target construct, theoretical composition model, context, and empirical evidence.

CSI therefore treats competing representations as hypotheses to be compared rather than assigning theoretical superiority in advance.

## Alternatives Considered

### Treat aggregation as intrinsically insufficient

Rejected because some collective constructs may be validly represented through additive, consensus, dispersion, or other compositional models.

### Privilege relational or temporal representations by definition

Rejected because richer representation does not guarantee greater construct validity or incremental value.

## Consequences

The canonical CSI definition should use qualified language such as "cannot always be adequately represented or inferred by aggregating" rather than a universal irreducibility claim.

Empirical studies should compare richer CSI representations against strong, theoretically justified simpler baselines.

## Backward Compatibility

Conceptually backward compatible. This decision refines the precision of existing wording rather than changing CSI's inferential target or foundational purpose.
